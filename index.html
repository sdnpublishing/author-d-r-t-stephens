D.R. T STEPHENS

D.R. T Stephens — often called 'The Renaissance Man' by his readers — has published over 150 books spanning the occult, world history, DIY crafts, nanotechnology, green witchcraft, cookery, and beyond. A genuine polymath with an insatiable curiosity and a whimsical sense of humour, he approaches every subject with the same intention: to make genuinely complex material feel like a conversation with a very well-read friend. His year-by-year history series and green witch library have each developed devoted readerships of their own.
